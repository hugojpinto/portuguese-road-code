Road Rules Lab

Micro-simulations that test two arguments about Portuguese and EU traffic rules. Each simulator puts the same drivers, with the same human-error rates, in the same random traffic under each rule set, and compares crashes, near misses, stress and delay.

Highway: keep right vs speed lanes

Does the EU "always keep right" rule, with its constant returns to the right and flashing at middle-lane drivers, cause more lane changes, stress and crashes than lanes with increasing speed from right to left?

Roundabout: PT 2014 vs lane integrity

Does "pick the lane by your exit, and the outer lane must exit" make two-lane roundabouts safer, or does it cause in-ring lane changes, one-sided queues and grey-area side impacts?
